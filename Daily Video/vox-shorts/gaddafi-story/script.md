## Facts (verified)

- 1 Sep 1969: Gaddafi (27) leads the Free Officers Movement in a bloodless coup, deposing King Idris while the king is abroad in Turkey.
- Libya at the time: one of the poorest countries in the world; massive oil reserves discovered in 1959, production ramping through the 1960s.
- 1970s: oil wealth transforms Libya; Gaddafi nationalizes oil, raises prices, funds Arab and African causes.
- The Green Book (3 vols, 1975-1981): Gaddafi's political philosophy — "Third Universal Theory," direct democracy via people's committees, rejection of both capitalism and communism.
- Jamahiriya ("state of the masses"): traditional government dismantled, replaced by a system of basic people's congresses.
- Great Man-Made River (1983+): massive pipeline project bringing fossil water from the Saharan aquifer to the coast — called the "Eighth Wonder of the World."
- Late 2000s: Gaddafi pushes the Pan-African gold dinar — a gold-backed currency to replace the dollar for African oil trade.
- 2011: Arab Spring reaches Libya; civil war; NATO intervention; Gaddafi captured and killed in Sirte, October 2011.
